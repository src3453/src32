UNSCII.F08 is a 2048-byte, 256-glyph CP437 bank. ASCII is unchanged in
encoding; CP437 graphics/control slots map to Unicode UNSCII glyphs.
NUL is blank; missing glyphs use question mark. Each row is MSB-left.
Missing mappings in this upstream revision: CP437 0x0F, 0x7F, 0xA9, 0xF9.
The artwork differs from the removed IBM CGA font.
