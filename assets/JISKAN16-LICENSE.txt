tools/unifont_hex_to_chr.py maps all 6,877 BDF glyphs from JIS X 0208
positions to Unicode via EUC-JP and overlays them on the Unifont BMP ROM.
This includes JIS punctuation, kana, kanji, symbols and full-width Latin,
Greek and Cyrillic characters; it does not replace unrelated Unicode slots.
The rows are stored unchanged (16 rows, two bytes per row, MSB on the left).
No CP932 compatibility aliases are added. Unifont supplies the remaining
slots, including U+FFFD and characters absent from this JIS X 0208 font.
The shared ROM feeds PCG mode 2, SGC GLYPH and Foundation's wide console font.

The Jiskan16 BDF is public domain. The combined ROM retains Unifont's
OFL-1.1 distribution terms; see assets/UNIFONT-LICENSE.txt.
